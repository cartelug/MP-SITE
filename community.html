Digital constituency office Prototype · browser only

Tell us what your community needs.

The production service is designed to ask four questions and return a reference number. This prototype demonstrates that journey, but it does not send anything to the constituency office yet.

The production journey
  • 01You submit and receive a reference number
  • 02The office classifies it by area and pillar
  • 03It is put to the responsible institution
  • 04The response is published, whatever it says
Prototype mode Nothing entered here is sent to the office.

Use sample information only. This build saves the demonstration on this browser so the local workflow can be reviewed. A secure backend, consent, retention and access rules are required before public use.

One place to find what is open.

Scholarships, training places, enterprise finance and public programmes are announced in a dozen places at once and missed by the people they were written for. This section is meant to end that.

Listings will be published here once each one has been verified with the institution offering it. An unverified opportunity wastes a journey that somebody could not afford to make.

Engagements

Where the office will be, before it gets there.

Community dialogues, field visits and programme days published in advance with the location, the time and the agenda — and a way to send a question ahead of the meeting, so the answer is ready when the room fills up.
