Data governance

No silent edits.

A dashboard that can be quietly rewritten is worth nothing. In the production system every public status change keeps a history: who changed it, when, why, and what evidence was added. That record is part of the publication, not an internal note.

For a site whose entire promise is accountability, the audit trail is the product. The office should be as easy to check as the projects it reports on.

  • 01Resident raises an issue and receives a reference number
  • 02Office classifies it by area, pillar and responsible institution
  • 03Action is recorded — letter, meeting, question, budget line
  • 04Institutional response is attached, in full, whatever it says
  • 05Delivery is verified on site before any status reads complete
  • 06Entry stays published, with its history intact
