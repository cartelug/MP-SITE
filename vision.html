Vision Budadiri County East

What this constituency is trying to become.

Not a slogan, and not a list of promises. A description precise enough that in ten years anyone can check whether it happened.

The vision
A productive, educated, connected and climate-resilient Budadiri East in which every young person, woman, child and vulnerable household can participate in and benefit from inclusive local development.

Adopted as the organising statement for the 2026–2031 term. Every pillar, project and dashboard entry on this site is written against it.

The ground it applies to

One county, two halves of a mountain.

Budadiri County East is the eastern half of Sironko District, in the Bugisu sub-region of eastern Uganda. It runs from the trading centres on the plain up onto the western shoulder of Mount Elgon, and the river that marks its western boundary is the same water that carries the hillside away in a bad season.

That geography is why the five pillars are not independent of each other. Coffee grows where the slope is steepest. The road that gets the coffee out is the road that washes away. The households furthest from the tarmac are the households furthest from a health centre.
