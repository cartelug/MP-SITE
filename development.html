Development Five pillars

Five priorities. One direction.

Each pillar names what should change for a household, what belongs under it, and what this office is responsible for chasing.

The same five priorities, in the national language.

NDP IV organises the country’s plan around production, human capital, infrastructure, climate and inclusive governance. The Budadiri pillars are those objectives written at the scale of a sub-county.

Budadiri East pillars mapped to NDP IV programme areas
PillarNDP IV programme areaWhat it should change locally
01 ProductiveAgro-industrialisation, production and value additionMore of the coffee value chain completed inside the constituency
02 EducatedHuman capital developmentSafe classrooms, and training places that lead to paid work
03 ConnectedSustainable infrastructure and transportRoads and bridges that hold through the wet season
04 Climate-resilientEnvironment, climate change and natural resourcesFewer households living and farming on unstable ground
05 InclusiveInclusive growth and governanceProgrammes counted by who they actually reached

This mapping is the office’s own reading of NDP IV, offered so that residents can check local priorities against the national plan. The source document is published in full on this site.
