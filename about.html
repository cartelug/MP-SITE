Why this site exists

Not a personality brochure.

A politician’s website usually exists to be admired. This one is built to be checked. It publishes the constituency’s priorities, the parliamentary record behind them, the status of what was promised, and a direct route for a resident to add something the office has missed.

Engineering and accounting are relevant here in a specific way: both are professions in which a claim that cannot be verified is worthless. That is the standard this platform is trying to hold.
