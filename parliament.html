The record Twelfth Parliament

From Budadiri to Parliament.

Every entry carries a date and a link to the official record. Nothing on this page asks to be taken on trust.

What this page becomes

A searchable constituency archive.

Every speech, question, committee action, motion, petition and ministerial response can be tagged to the pillar it affects and the sub-county it serves, then ingested from the official Parliament record rather than retyped.

The point is not volume. It is that a resident of Bumasifwa can filter for water, read what was asked on their behalf, and see the answer that came back — including when no answer came back.
